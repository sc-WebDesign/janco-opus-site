What we fix

Domestic appliances, and the electrics that feed them

Repairs first. If a machine genuinely is not worth mending, we will say so rather than take the money.

Washing machines

The bulk of the work, and has been for decades. Drums, bearings, pumps, motors, seals, and the leaks that only show up on a spin cycle.

Tumble dryers

Not heating, not turning, taking three cycles to dry a load. Stripped down and diagnosed at your address, and taken to the workshop only if it needs the bench.

Ovens and grills

Elements, thermostats and the grill that stopped working while the oven carried on. Built-in and freestanding.

Dishwashers

Not draining, not filling, not cleaning properly. Same approach: find the fault first, then tell you honestly whether it is worth doing.

Fitting a new one

Installed, levelled, plumbed and connected properly. If it needs a new power cable to be done safely, that gets done on the same visit.

Taking the old one away

Lifted out, taken with us and dropped at a recycling centre. Customers mention this more than almost anything else, so it is worth saying plainly: you are not left with it.

The other half of the name

It says Electrical Services on the van for a reason

The full name of the business is Janco Domestic Electrical Services, and that second word does real work. An appliance is only as good as what it is plugged into.

When a customer had an old electric oven taken out and a new one put in, the write-up mentions the part most engineers would have left for somebody else: new power cables were run for it as part of the same visit. One trip, one bill, one person responsible for the whole thing working.

If your job involves the supply as well as the appliance, say so when you ring so the right kit comes out with us.

Repair, don’t replace

Questions

Before you ring

Is it worth repairing, or should I just buy a new one?

That is the question the whole business is built around, and the honest answer is that it depends on the fault. A pump or an element is almost always worth doing. A failed drum on a cheap machine often is not. You will get a straight answer either way, including when the answer costs us the job.

Will you take the old machine away?

Yes. It comes out, goes on the van and gets dropped at a recycling centre. Customers mention this in reviews more than almost anything else, which suggests plenty of people have been left standing over a dead appliance by somebody else.

Which makes do you work on?

All makes and models is the long-standing claim, and sixty-two years of it makes that fairly credible. Bosch, Zanussi and Hotpoint have been regulars throughout. If you have something unusual, mention the model number when you ring.

Do you have to take it away?

Usually not. Most faults are diagnosed and fixed where the machine stands. Bench work is for the jobs that genuinely need it, and those come back quickly.

What will it cost?

There are no prices on this page, deliberately. The fault dictates the part and the part dictates the price, so anything printed here would be a guess. What reviews consistently say is that the final number is fair and that nobody takes liberties. Ring and ask.
